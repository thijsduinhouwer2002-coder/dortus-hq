## Titel
Too big for your printer? One click splits it to fit | Blender

## Tekst
```
Model too big for your printer bed? Click Cuts splits it for you, in Blender.
1. Click your model and pick your printer.
2. Press Split to fit. Every part is cut to fit your bed.
3. Pins, pin holes and part numbers go on the cut faces. Each part gets a check when it fits.

Print the fit test first: every printer is different.
Click Cuts, add-on for Blender: <link, hoofdchat>

Model: Horse Statue 01 by Poly Haven (CC0).

#blender #3dprinting #b3d #blenderaddon
```
