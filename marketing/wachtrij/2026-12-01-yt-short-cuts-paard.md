## Titel
Too big for your printer? One click splits it to fit | Blender

## Tekst
```
Model too big for your printer bed? Click Cuts splits it for you.
1. Click your model and pick your printer.
2. Press Split to fit. Every cut is planned so each part fits your bed.
3. Pins, pin holes and part numbers are added on the cut faces. Each part turns green when it fits.

Print the fit test block first: every printer is different.
Click Cuts, for Blender, on Gumroad: <link na de lancering, hoofdchat>

Model: Horse Statue 01 by Poly Haven (CC0).
```
