## Titel
One click turns your rigged figure into printable parts 🔥 | Blender

## Tekst
```
Your figure has a rig? Let Click Joints Pro read it.
1. Click your figure, then Figure from rig.
2. It cuts the figure into parts, each with its own joint. Knees and elbows get a hinge with an end stop.
3. Bend the knee: it stops at the limit from your rig. Past the stop, red shows where parts would hit.

Print the Fit test strip first: every printer is different.
```
