## Titel
Split a figure into printable parts in Blender

## Tekst
```
Split a figure into printable parts in Blender. Step 1: select your rigged figure and click Figure from rig. Step 2: every joint is cut in, hinges for elbows and knees, ball joints for neck, shoulders and hips. Step 3: turn on Exploded view and check every part. Step 4: export 3MF, one file per part. Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet.

Real recording in Blender 5.3 with our add-on Click Joints Pro 1.1, on a MakeHuman figure (CC0).
Click Joints Pro: type dortus.com/yt in your browser.

#blender #3dprinting #b3d #blendertips #shorts
```
