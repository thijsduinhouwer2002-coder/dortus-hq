## Titel
One click: rig to 10 printable parts in Blender

## Tekst
```
One button turns a rigged figure into printable parts: hinges with an end stop at elbows and knees, ball joints at shoulders, hips and neck. 9 joints, 10 parts, and every joint shows its range in degrees. Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet. Print a test joint first, every printer is different.

Real recording in Blender 5.3 with our add-on Click Joints Pro 1.1, on a MakeHuman figure (CC0).
Click Joints Pro: type dortus.com/yt in your browser.
```
