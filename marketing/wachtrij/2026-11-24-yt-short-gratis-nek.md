## Titel
Free Blender add-on: ball joint neck for a 3D printed figure

## Tekst
```
Our free Blender add-on Click Joints: put the 3D cursor on the neck, press Place joint, and the figure gets a ball and socket. The ring shows the fit, and it shows how far the head can tilt, in degrees, red past that. Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet. Print a test joint first, every printer is different.

Real recording in Blender 5.3 with Click Joints 0.1.1, free on extensions.blender.org (search "Click Joints"), on a MakeHuman figure (CC0). Want hinges with end stops and a whole figure from your rig? Click Joints Pro: type dortus.com/yt in your browser.
```
