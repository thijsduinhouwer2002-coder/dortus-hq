# tip-glad: voorleestekst voor ElevenLabs (Max - Witty Commercial Actor, Eleven v4), zinnen in volgorde.
# Lees precies deze zinnen voor, in deze volgorde. Zet de mp3 als commercie/filmpjes/regisseur-6/tip-glad-elevenlabs.mp3.
1. Does your model look smooth in Blender, but blocky when you print it?
2. Shade Smooth only changes how Blender draws it. Your slicer gets the flat faces.
3. Right click and pick Shade Flat to see what you will really print.
4. Those flat faces are what ends up in your print.
5. Open the Modifier properties and click Add Modifier.
6. Choose Generate, then Subdivision Surface.
7. Set Levels Viewport to two.
8. Hover over the modifier, and press Control A to apply it.
9. Now it looks round, even with flat shading. Ninety six faces became one thousand four hundred forty real faces.
10. It gets a little smaller, so check the size before you export.
11. Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet. Save this for your next print.
12. More Blender tips for three D printing, at dortus dot com slash Y T.
