Want your name on a 3D print? Here is how, in Blender.
Open the Add menu and click Text.
It is only one millimeter tall. Press S, type twenty, Enter.
Press Tab, delete the word, type your name, then Tab again.
Now look from the side. The text is flat, zero thickness, so there is nothing to print.
Open Object, Convert, Mesh.
Press Tab, A, then E, type three, Enter.
Now every letter is three millimeters thick. Press N and check the Dimensions.
Export it as STL and open it in your slicer.
Every printer is different, so print a small test first. Save this for your next print.
Make your prints poseable: Click Joints Pro, on Gumroad.
