Will your print fall over? Check it in Blender before you print.
Open Object, Set Origin, Origin to Center of Mass, Volume.
Now look from the front. View, Viewpoint, Front.
The orange dot is the balance point. It sits outside the foot, so this tips over.
To fix it, press Tab, then three. From below, click the bottom face.
Press S, type three, Enter. The foot is now three times wider.
Set the origin again. The dot is now inside the foot.
A wider foot or less lean keeps your print standing.
Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet. Save this for your next print.
