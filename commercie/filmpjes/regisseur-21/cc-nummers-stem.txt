Twelve printed parts, and no idea where?
Click Cuts engraves a number into every cut face.
Each face shows its own part number and the part it joins.
It is sunk into the cut, so the outside of your model stays clean.
And it sits where there is room, not on top of a pin hole.
Smaller faces get smaller numbers, so the text still fits on the face.
Open More settings, step three, to set the depth: 0.6 millimetres by default.
Parts are numbered from bottom to top, so you build up in order.
Export parts, and the files are named piece zero one, piece zero two, in that order.
Not print tested by us yet. Print the fit test first, then the parts.
Click Cuts for Blender. Link in the description.
