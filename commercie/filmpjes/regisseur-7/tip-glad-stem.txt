# Bijgewerkt Regisseur run 25 (24 nov): slotzin naar Gumroad, geen 'not print tested' meer.

Does your model look smooth in Blender, but blocky when you print it?
Shade Smooth only changes how Blender draws it. Your slicer gets the flat faces.
Right click and pick Shade Flat to see what you will really print.
Those flat faces are what ends up in your print.
Open the Modifier properties and click Add Modifier.
Choose Generate, then Subdivision Surface.
Set Levels Viewport to two.
Hover over the modifier, and press Control A to apply it.
Now it looks round, even with flat shading. Ninety six faces became one thousand four hundred forty real faces.
It gets a little smaller, so check the size before you export.
Every printer is different, so print a small test first. Save this for your next print.
Make your prints poseable: Click Joints Pro, on Gumroad.
