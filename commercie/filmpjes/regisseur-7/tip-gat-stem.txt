# Bijgewerkt Regisseur run 25 (24 nov): slotzin naar Gumroad, geen 'not print tested' meer.

Does your slicer say your model has holes?
Blender can find them for you in a few seconds, before you print.
Select your model, press Tab for Edit Mode, then two for edge select.
Press Alt A, so nothing is selected.
Open Select. Select All by Trait. Non-manifold.
Every edge that is not closed lights up. Here, a hole in the head of the pawn.
Press F to fill the hole with a new face.
Run the check again with Shift, Control, Alt, M. Nothing lights up, so the mesh is closed.
Every printer is different, so print a small test first. Save this for your next print.
Make your prints poseable: Click Joints Pro, on Gumroad.
