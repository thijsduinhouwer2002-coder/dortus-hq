Is your model too big for your printer?
Check it in Blender, before you slice.
First, in Scene properties, set Unit Scale to zero point zero zero one, and Length to millimeters.
Press N. Under Dimensions you see the real size: three hundred ten millimeters tall.
This box is a print bed of two hundred twenty by two hundred twenty by two hundred fifty millimeters. The pawn is too tall for it.
Hover over the view, press S, type zero point seven, and press Enter.
Now it is two hundred seventeen millimeters tall, and it fits inside the bed.
Every printer is different, so print a small test first. Save this for your next print.
Make your prints poseable: Click Joints Pro, on Gumroad.
