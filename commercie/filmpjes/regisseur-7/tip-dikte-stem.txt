# Bijgewerkt Regisseur run 25 (24 nov): slotzin naar Gumroad, geen 'not print tested' meer.

Is your model just one paper thin surface?
A wall with zero thickness has nothing to print.
No need to model a second wall by hand. One modifier does it.
Open the Modifier properties and click Add Modifier.
Choose Generate, then Solidify.
Click the Thickness field, type two, and press Enter.
Now the edge is a real wall, two millimeters thick, all the way around.
Offset minus one puts the wall on the inside, so the outside keeps its size.
And Even Thickness keeps the wall the same on sharp corners.
Every printer is different, so print a small test first. Save this for your next print.
Make your prints poseable: Click Joints Pro, on Gumroad.
