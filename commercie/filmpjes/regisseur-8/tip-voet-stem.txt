Does the bottom edge of your print bulge out, like a little lip?
That is elephant foot. The first layer gets squished wider than the rest.
Give the bottom edge a small slope in Blender, before you slice.
Press Tab for Edit Mode, then three for face select.
Open View, Viewpoint, Bottom. Then click the bottom face.
Press Control B, type zero point five, and press Enter.
Now the bottom edge has a slope of half a millimeter, all the way around.
Print guides suggest zero point two to zero point five millimeters. The wider first layer fills that slope.
Every printer is different, so print a small test first. Save this for your next print.
Make your prints poseable: Click Joints Pro, on Gumroad.
