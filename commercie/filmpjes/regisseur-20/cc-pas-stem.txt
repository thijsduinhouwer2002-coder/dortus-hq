Do your printed pins never fit?
Every printer and filament prints a hole a little different.
So open More settings, and go to step three: pins and numbers.
Press Make fit test. It uses your own pin size from the panel.
You get a small test block with four holes, plus four pins.
Each hole has a different clearance, engraved right next to it.
Print it, try your pin, and click the hole that fits.
It remembers that clearance for this printer and this filament.
Switch printer or filament, and the saved clearance comes back by itself.
Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet. That is why you print this first.
Click Cuts for Blender. Link in the description.
