Is your model secretly in two pieces? Check it in Blender.
Press Tab, point at the top, and press L.
Only the top lights up. Blender sees two loose parts.
The seam has forty eight double points, so the edge is open.
Press A. Then press M and pick By Distance.
Blender removed forty eight points. The seam is closed.
Press Alt A, then L again. Now the whole vase lights up.
Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet. Save this before your next export.
