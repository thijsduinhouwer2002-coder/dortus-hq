Model too big for your print bed?
Pick your printer from the list, or type in your own bed size.
Then press Split to fit, the one big button in the panel.
Open model? It makes a watertight copy first, in the same click.
It cuts the model into the fewest parts that each fit your bed.
Parts that touch never share a color, so you see every cut.
Every cut gets pins and pin holes, with a clearance you can tune to your printer.
The panel tells you in one line: all parts fit the bed.
Then export every part and the pins as S T L or three M F, in millimeters.
Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet. Print the fit test first.
Click Cuts for Blender. Link in the description.
