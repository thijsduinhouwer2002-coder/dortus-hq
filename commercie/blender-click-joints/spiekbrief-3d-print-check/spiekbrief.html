Blender 3D print check

6 fixes before you export to your slicer. No add-on needed. Screenshots from Blender 5.3.

1. Is it the right size?

Scene Properties > Units: Unit Scale 0.001, Length Millimeters. Press N and read Dimensions; compare with your print bed and scale with S if it does not fit.

2. Any holes?

Tab, 2 for edge select, Alt A. Select > Select All by Trait > Non Manifold. Every open edge lights up; press F to fill a hole, then check again until nothing lights up.

3. Any faces inside out?

Overlays > Face Orientation: red means you see the back of a face. Tab, A, then Shift N (Recalculate Outside) until no red is left.

4. Do the walls have thickness?

A surface with zero thickness has nothing to print. Add Modifier > Generate > Solidify, Thickness 2 mm, Offset -1, Even Thickness on.

5. Will the first layer bulge?

Against elephant foot: Tab, 3 for face select, click the bottom face, Ctrl B, 0.5, Enter. A 0.5 mm slope on the bottom edge (print guides suggest 0.2 to 0.5 mm).

6. Smooth on screen, smooth in print?

Shade Smooth only changes how Blender draws it. Right click > Shade Flat to see the real faces, add a Subdivision Surface (Levels 2) and apply it with Ctrl A. Check the size again.
