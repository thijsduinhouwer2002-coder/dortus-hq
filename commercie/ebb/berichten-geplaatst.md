**A. "Does it work on my watch?" / "Why not on my Venu Sq / Instinct / Forerunner X?"** (lijst van de horloges overgenomen uit dortus.com/ebb/, GEZIEN 17:55 UTC)
```
Thanks for asking. Ebb is built for round watches, because the watch face is drawn as a porthole on a small sea. Right now it runs on 28 models: fenix 7 and 8, epix Pro (Gen 2), Forerunner 165, 255, 265, 570, 955, 965 and 970, Venu 2, 3, 3S and 4, and vivoactive 5 and 6. The store page shows the exact list for your watch; if yours is not there, Ebb will not install on it. Which watch do you have? If many people ask for the same one, I'll look at it for a next version, but I can't promise it.
```

**B. "Is there a trial?"**
```
Ebb has no trial. The closest thing is the store's 48-hour return window, see the store page. A free calm-only version, Ebb Calm, is waiting for Garmin's review; I'll post it here when it's live. Breath hold rounds, My pattern and the 24 sea animals are only in Ebb.
```

**C. "Is this safe? Breath holds can be dangerous."**
```
Fair question. Ebb is for practice on land only: sit or lie down, never in or near water, never while driving. It reminds you before every session. From 3:00 the watch shows "breathe" and buzzes every 30 seconds, and a hold stops by itself after five minutes. Animals come from sessions and weeks in a row, never from holding longer, so there is nothing to win by pushing. Feel dizzy or tingly? Stop and breathe normally. Not if pregnant, under 18, or with epilepsy, heart or blood pressure problems. It is not a medical device and has no CO2 or O2 tables.
```

**D. "AI wrote this? Then I don't trust it." (of een andere klacht over AI)**
```
That's a fair concern. AI agents write the code at Dortus; I decide what it does, check the screens and test it. It was compiled for all 28 supported watches and tested in the Connect IQ simulator; it has not been tested on every watch model. If something doesn't work on yours, tell me here or at thijs@dortus.com and I'll look at it.
```

**E. "Does it need a phone or an account? What data does it collect?"**
```
No phone app, no extra account and no unlock code. Ebb collects nothing: sessions, animals and settings stay on your watch. Only a saved activity goes to Garmin Connect, under Garmin's privacy policy. No analytics, no ads. More: https://dortus.com/ebb/privacy.html
```
(Dit is het enige toegestane antwoord op een privacyvraag: het herhaalt alleen de gepubliceerde winkeltekst en verwijst naar de privacypagina. Elke vervolgvraag over privacy of wetten: niet beantwoorden, bovenaan de lijst van Thijs.)
