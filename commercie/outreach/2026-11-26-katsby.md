```
Subject: Your "Connect 3D Prints" tutorial, and a free license for a Blender tool

Hi,

In "Best Way To Connect 3D Prints" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mJybrr2EcA4) you show how to design a custom joint by hand so printed parts fit together. We just released a Blender add-on that does the cutting and the connectors for you, and I thought of that video.

Click Cuts splits a model into parts that fit your printer bed. Pick your printer (or type your own bed size), press Split to fit, and every cut gets round pins and pin holes, or, where they fit, holes for disc magnets, plus an engraved part number. Each part turns green when it fits, is watertight and has enough wall around its holes. You export all parts and pins as STL or 3MF in millimetres. It works in Blender 4.2 and newer.

It also gives you one small fit test block with your pin at four clearances. You pick the one that fits and Click Cuts keeps it for that printer. Print that block first: every printer is different.

I'd like to give you a free license. No obligation at all: if you don't like it, just don't mention it. If you ever do show it, please say you got it for free.
```
