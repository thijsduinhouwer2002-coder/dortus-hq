```
Hi Jim,

Your "New CG software you may have missed" round-up of 25 October covered smaller Blender add-ons like Marionette, so here is one that may fit a future round-up:
https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/10/new-cg-software-you-may-have-missed-25-october-2026/

Click Cuts is a new Blender add-on for 3D printing, released on 27 November. You pick your printer (9 presets, or type your own bed size), press Split to fit, and it cuts the selected model into parts that fit the bed, with pins and pin holes, or holes for disc magnets, and a part number engraved on the cut faces. Each part turns green when it fits, is watertight and has enough wall around its holes, or orange or red with the reason. A small fit test block, printed at four clearances, sets the pin clearance for your own printer. All parts export as STL or 3MF in millimetres.

It is made for busts, cosplay props, terrain and statues that are too big for one print.

Price: $19 (introductory price until 14 December). Blender 4.2 and newer.
Product page with screenshots: https://duinhouwer8.gumroad.com/l/click-cuts?utm_source=cgchannel&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=click-cuts

Happy to send a free license if you would like to try it.

Thanks,
Thijs
Dortus
```
