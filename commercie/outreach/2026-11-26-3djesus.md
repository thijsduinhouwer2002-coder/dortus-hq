Hi Steve,

Your "STL TOO BIG for Your 3D Printer? This FREE Tool Splits It!" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rEAvLUnljDs) and the cosplay helmet video are about the exact problem we just built a tool for, so I wanted to show it to you.

Click Cuts is a Blender add-on that splits a model into parts that fit your printer bed. Pick your printer (or type your own bed size), press Split to fit, and every cut gets round pins and pin holes, or, where they fit, holes for disc magnets, plus an engraved part number. Each part turns green when it fits, is watertight and has enough wall around its holes. You export all parts and pins as STL or 3MF in millimetres. It works in Blender 4.2 and newer, and Blender itself is free.

It also gives you one small fit test block with your pin at four clearances. You pick the one that fits and Click Cuts keeps it for that printer. Print that block first: every printer is different.
