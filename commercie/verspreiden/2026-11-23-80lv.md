Hi 80 Level team,

A news tip in case it fits your Blender coverage.

Click Joints Pro is a Blender add-on that turns a rigged character into printable parts that move. Pick the rigged figure and press one button: elbows and knees get a hinge with an end stop, shoulders, hips and neck get a ball joint. If a bone has a Limit Rotation constraint, the hinge stops at that angle, and every joint shows its bend range in degrees on the model before anything is printed. It reads Rigify, Mixamo, Auto-Rig Pro, Unreal, Character Creator, VRoid, 3ds Max Biped and Daz bone names, and exports STL or 3MF. It works best on figures about 200 mm tall or more with separate arms and legs, in Blender 4.5 or newer.

So a game character that is already rigged can become a poseable figure without cutting the joints by hand. It is not print tested by us yet, so we tell users to print a test joint first.

There is also a free version with ball and pin joints on Blender Extensions (GPL):
https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/click-joints/

Click Joints Pro is $19 on Gumroad, 25% off from November 27 to December 1:

Attached: a short GIF of a Pro hinge bending through its range (0 to 115 degrees), a screenshot of a rigged figure with all nine joints and their ranges (Pro), and a render of the free version's ball joints. Happy to answer questions or send more images.
