```
Click Joints Pro is a Blender add-on that turns a rigged character into printable parts that move. Pick the rigged figure and press one button, Figure from rig: elbows and knees get a hinge with an end stop, shoulders, hips and neck get a ball joint. If a bone has a Limit Rotation constraint, the hinge stops at that angle (30 to 150 degrees). Next to each joint you see how far it bends, in degrees, and past the stop the part turns red. It recognises Rigify, Mixamo, Auto-Rig Pro, Unreal, Character Creator, VRoid, Biped and Daz bone names. Export is STL or 3MF, one file per part.

Version 1.2 adds a Fit test strip: five joints from 0.10 to 0.30 mm clearance, each with its number, so you know which gap fits your printer before you print the figure.

Click Joints Pro costs from $19 on Gumroad (25% off from November 27 to December 1) and is also on Superhive. It works best on figures about 200 mm tall or more, in Blender 4.5 or newer. There is also a free, open source (GPL) version, Click Joints, with ball and pin joints and a bend range check, on Blender Extensions: https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/click-joints/

Walkthrough video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4-3l3MZZWPc
Gumroad: https://duinhouwer8.gumroad.com/l/click-joints-pro

Measured in Blender. Made by Dortus.
```
