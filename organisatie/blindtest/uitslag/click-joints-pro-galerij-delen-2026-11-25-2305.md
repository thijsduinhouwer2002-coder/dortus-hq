## alle delen van een organisch figuur op de printplaat, export: GESLAAGD

Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/ontwerp/pro-galerij/beelden/delen-mens-2400x1200.jpg

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 1.5, ChatGPT 1.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 1, eerste keus alle delen van een organisch figuur op de printplaat, export. Over ons: It explicitly caters to making action figures with articulated joints like knee hinges, and the inclusion of automatic clearance and 1-click exporting is a massive time-saver. Beter maken: Showcase a wider variety of articulation types, such as double-jointed elbows or butterfly shoulders, to fully sell the 'action figure' capability.
- Ronde 1, ChatGPT: wij plek 1, eerste keus alle delen van een organisch figuur op de printplaat, export. Over ons: Promises ready‑to‑print separated parts with built‑in ball/hinge joints, defined 0.20 mm clearance, and one‑click multi‑file export—exactly the full pipeline I need. Beter maken: Make it explicit that the 0.20 mm clearance is user‑adjustable per joint and show the UI for that.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 2, eerste keus Cleave (galerij: Connectors that fit). Over ons: The promo art looks incredibly amateurish, but offering true mechanical hinges with end-stops and automatic batch export perfectly addresses the real-world nightmare of making posable toys. Beter maken: Demonstrate the tool working on a high-resolution, professional action figure sculpt to prove it works on complex geometry and not just basic low-poly shapes.
- Ronde 2, ChatGPT: wij plek 1, eerste keus alle delen van een organisch figuur op de printplaat, export. Over ons: Promises automatic parting with ball/hinge joints already inside and one‑click multi-file export—exactly what I need for articulated figures. Beter maken: Show a printed articulated figure made with it, proving the joints click and hold poses.

Modellen: Gemini = gemini-3.1-pro-preview, ChatGPT = gpt-5
