# Blinde test: Hats of the Fallen, Steam header v11a (echt spelbeeld onderaan een bureaublad met spreadsheet, nieuw pixellogo) en v11b (v10 in 3D met alleen het nieuwe pixellogo, zonder pilletje) tegen Rusty's Retirement, Spirit City: Lofi Sessions en Desktop Defender

Rol voor de rechters: You play cozy idle games on PC while you work or study. You want a small game that lives on your desktop or at the bottom of your screen. You are scrolling the Steam store and only see the small header image of each game. Pick the game you would click on first.

Concurrenten: [Rusty's Retirement](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2666510/1b9c3e5c1a8a08354bed63fbeed798fcec74aff9/header.jpg), [Spirit City: Lofi Sessions](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2113850/dde323de28b5db2af88e7fda53ed3c4dc34280bf/header.jpg), [Desktop Defender](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/3772810/a2cac908cf58a8017497900dc3db474823a52ae5/header_alt_assets_3.jpg)

Geslaagd = bij elke rechter gemiddeld plek 2 of beter (van 4), over 2 rondes met een andere volgorde.

## Hats header v11a: NIET GESLAAGD

Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/ontwerp/steam/capsule-v2/header-v11a-920x430.png?v=11a

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 3.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Rusty's Retirement. Over ons: It has an interesting desktop overlay concept shown with the spreadsheet, but the visual art style looks a bit messy and cheap. Beter maken: Refine the character and environment sprite art to look more polished and charming rather than cluttered.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Rusty's Retirement. Over ons: The spreadsheet integration is a clever concept for working users, but the cartoonish character art looks a bit cheap and confusing. Beter maken: Clean up the visual hierarchy so it's instantly obvious how the idle gameplay interacts with productivity windows.

## Hats header v11b: NIET GESLAAGD

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 3.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Spirit City: Lofi Sessions. Over ons: It looks playful and interacts with the taskbar, but the art style feels a bit too chaotic and juvenile for my taste. - Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Rusty's Retirement. Over ons: The art style looks a bit messy and cheap, and the taskbar overlay makes it confusing to understand what kind of game it actually is. Beter maken: Clean up the thumbnail art to better highlight the idle mechanic instead of showing a confusing taskbar mockup.
