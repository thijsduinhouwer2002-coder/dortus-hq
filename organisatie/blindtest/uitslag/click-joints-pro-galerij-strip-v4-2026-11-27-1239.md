Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/ontwerp/pro-galerij/beelden/strip-v4-2400x1200.jpg

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 2.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 2, eerste keus Cleave (galerij: Connectors that fit). Over ons: Focuses on useful practical tests for print clearance, specifically addressing printer tolerances for functional joints. Beter maken: Include real-world application examples on actual action figure limbs rather than just abstract cylinders.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 2, eerste keus Cleave (galerij: Connectors that fit). Over ons: Focuses heavily on precise clearance testing for printer calibration, which is essential for functional action figure joints. Beter maken: Show how these tested clearances apply to complex action figure poses in a real scene.

## Strip v3 (gekeurd, Daan #215): GESLAAGD

Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/ontwerp/pro-galerij/beelden/strip-v3-2400x1200.jpg

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 2.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Split 3D Print (enige galerijbeeld). Over ons: Focusing purely on clearance test fits is useful for calibration, but it's too niche compared to actual cutting tools. Beter maken: Include a demonstration of how these tested tolerances integrate into a fully cut character joint.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 1, eerste keus Strip v3 (gekeurd, Daan #215). Over ons: Directly addresses the biggest headache in 3D printing movable joints with exact clearance testing. Beter maken: Show more variety in the joint shapes beyond just balls and basic hinges.
