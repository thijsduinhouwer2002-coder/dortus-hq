## alle delen op de printplaat, export: NIET GESLAAGD

Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/ontwerp/pro-galerij/beelden/delen-2400x1200.jpg

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 3.0, ChatGPT 1.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Cleave (galerij: Connectors that fit). Over ons: Highlighting built-in clearances, export batching, and specific articulation joints is perfect for making action figures, though the example model is quite simplistic. Beter maken: Demonstrate these articulated joints being applied to a detailed, high-poly organic character to prove the add-on isn't limited to low-poly, blocky geometry.
- Ronde 1, ChatGPT: wij plek 1, eerste keus alle delen op de printplaat, export. Over ons: Promises parts already separated with functional joints, stated 0.20 mm clearance, and one‑click export to per‑part STL/3MF—clearly print‑ready. Beter maken: Demonstrate on an organic character and include a photo/clip of the printed joints moving.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus CatPin (galerij: More tools 1.0.7). Over ons: The one-click batch export to separate files is a massive time-saver and the defined 0.20mm clearance is great, though the showcase feels limited to simplistic robotic shapes. Beter maken: Demonstrate the tool's effectiveness and joint generation on complex organic models rather than just low-poly, hard-surface toys.
- Ronde 2, ChatGPT: wij plek 1, eerste keus alle delen op de printplaat, export. Over ons: Clearly targets articulated figures with built‑in ball/hinge joints, 0.20 mm clearance, and one‑click multi‑file export. Beter maken: Demonstrate on an organic character and show real printed joints moving smoothly.
