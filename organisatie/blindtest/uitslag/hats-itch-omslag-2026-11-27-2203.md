# Blinde test: Hats of the Fallen, itch.io-omslag 630x500 uit een echt beeld van het spel, v2 (hol op ware grootte, groot logo) en v3 (hol 1,25x), na v1 (1,75x) NIET GESLAAGD, tegen Rusty's Retirement, Spirit City: Lofi Sessions en Desktop Defender

Rol voor de rechters: You play cozy idle games on PC while you work or study. You want a small game that lives on your desktop or at the bottom of your screen. You are browsing a game store and only see the cover image of each game. Pick the game you would click on first.

Concurrenten: [Rusty's Retirement](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2666510/1b9c3e5c1a8a08354bed63fbeed798fcec74aff9/header.jpg), [Spirit City: Lofi Sessions](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2113850/dde323de28b5db2af88e7fda53ed3c4dc34280bf/header.jpg), [Desktop Defender](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/3772810/a2cac908cf58a8017497900dc3db474823a52ae5/header_alt_assets_3.jpg)

Geslaagd = bij elke rechter gemiddeld plek 2 of beter (van 4), over 2 rondes met een andere volgorde.

## Hats itch omslag v2: NIET GESLAAGD

Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/producten/steam-hol/itch/omslag-v2-630x500.png?v=2

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 3.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Spirit City: Lofi Sessions. Over ons: The generic mobile-game 3D aesthetic looks cheap and unappealing for a PC desktop experience. Beter maken: Upgrade the 3D art style to look less like a generic hyper-casual mobile app.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Spirit City: Lofi Sessions. Over ons: The bright cartoon aesthetic looks like a cheap mobile game port rather than a relaxing desktop companion. Beter maken: Adopt a softer, less saturated art style that feels more native to a PC desktop.

## Hats itch omslag v3: NIET GESLAAGD

Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/producten/steam-hol/itch/omslag-v3-630x500.png?v=3

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 3.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Spirit City: Lofi Sessions. Over ons: The cartoon art style feels a bit too generic and mobile-game-like, offering no clear indication that it makes a good background PC companion. Beter maken: Adopt a cleaner, more unique art style that distances itself from generic mobile puzzle games.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Spirit City: Lofi Sessions. Over ons: The colorful blob characters and hat mechanics seem mildly amusing, but the banner layout feels a bit cheap and mobile-game-like. Beter maken: Refine the key art to look less like a generic mobile app store asset and more like a dedicated PC title.
