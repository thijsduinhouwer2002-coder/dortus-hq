Rol voor de rechters: You play cozy idle games on PC while you work or study. You want a small game that lives on your desktop or at the bottom of your screen. You are scrolling the Steam store and only see the small header image of each game. Pick the game you would click on first.

Concurrenten: [Rusty's Retirement](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2666510/1b9c3e5c1a8a08354bed63fbeed798fcec74aff9/header.jpg), [Spirit City: Lofi Sessions](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2113850/dde323de28b5db2af88e7fda53ed3c4dc34280bf/header.jpg), [Desktop Defender](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/3772810/a2cac908cf58a8017497900dc3db474823a52ae5/header_alt_assets_3.jpg)

Geslaagd = bij elke rechter gemiddeld plek 2 of beter (van 4), over 2 rondes met een andere volgorde.

## Hats header v7: NIET GESLAAGD

Kandidaat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thijsduinhouwer2002-coder/dortus-hq/main/ontwerp/steam/capsule-v2/header-v7-920x430.png?v=7c

Gemiddelde plek: Gemini 3.0

- Ronde 1, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Rusty's Retirement. Over ons: The taskbar integration concept is brilliant and funny, but the 3D art style looks a bit cheap and chaotic for a relaxing companion. Beter maken: Refine the 3D models and lighting to look less like a mobile asset flip and more like a polished indie title.
- Ronde 2, Gemini: wij plek 3, eerste keus Spirit City: Lofi Sessions. Over ons: The concept of a taskbar basement sounds fun for multitasking, but the 3D art style looks a bit cheap and unpolished. Beter maken: Adopt a cleaner, more charming 2D pixel art style to make it feel less cluttered on the taskbar.
