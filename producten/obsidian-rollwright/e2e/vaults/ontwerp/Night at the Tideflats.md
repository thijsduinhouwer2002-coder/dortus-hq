# Night at the Tideflats

*Session 12 · the docks of Harrow, past midnight*

> [!handout] A note nailed to the pier
> Whoever lights the lantern again, the sea will take its due. Leave it dark.

Fog rolls in off the Grey Bay. Somebody lit the lantern anyway, and now something answers from the tideflats.

**Initiative** `dice: 1d20 + 3`

> [!beast] Dire wolf of the tideflats
> A grey shape lopes out of the fog, fur matted with salt, eyes like wet coins.
>
> **Bite** `dice: 1d20 + 6|text(Attack)` for `dice: 2d6 + 4|text(Damage)`

> [!trap] Rusted jaw trap
> Old iron, hidden under the nets. It has been waiting a long time.
>
> **Dexterity save** `dice: 1d20 + 1|text(Dex save)`

The lantern flickers. Out on the water, a second light opens its eyes.

**Perception** `dice: 1d20 + 2|text(Perception)`
