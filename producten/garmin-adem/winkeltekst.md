## Naam en ondertitel
Ebb: breath hold & breathwork

## Korte tekst (eerste regels in de winkel)
Breathing rounds and calm patterns on your wrist, drawn as water. Your screen fills as you breathe in, empties as you breathe out, and turns into a dive when you hold your breath.

## Lange tekst
Ebb turns your round watch face into a porthole.

ROUNDS
A set number of deep breaths at a steady pace, then breathe out and hold for as long as you like. Press any button to stop the hold, then take one full breath and hold it to recover. The hold is a dive: the water gets deeper every 15 seconds, bubbles rise, and a fish, a jellyfish, a turtle and a whale pass by at 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 and 3:00. The sea log keeps every animal you have met.

CALM BREATHING
Box 4-4-4-4, 4-7-8, a slow 5.5 in and 5.5 out, and one pattern of your own.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
After each session you get a dive profile: one V per round, as deep as your hold. Your deepest round is marked, and a new personal best shows in coral. The tide screen shows your best hold per day over 8 weeks.

WORKS WITH YOUR EYES CLOSED
Every phase has its own vibration, so you can breathe without looking.

SAVED AS AN ACTIVITY
Each session is saved as a breathing activity in Garmin Connect, one lap per round, with your hold time per lap and your best hold.

ON THE WATCH
Settings: breaths per round, pace, rounds, recovery hold, your own pattern, vibration on or off.

HONEST
One payment in the Garmin store. No subscription, no code by e-mail, no account, no phone app, no data sent anywhere. We will never ask you for a review.

SAFETY
Sit or lie down. Never practise breath holds in or near water, or while driving. Feel dizzy? Stop and breathe normally. Ebb is not a medical device.
