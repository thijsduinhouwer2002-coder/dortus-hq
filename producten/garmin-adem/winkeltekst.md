## Description (English)

Ebb turns your round watch face into a porthole. Breath hold rounds, box breathing and calm breathing patterns on your wrist, drawn as water: the screen fills as you breathe in, empties as you breathe out, and becomes a dive when you hold your breath.

BREATH HOLD ROUNDS
• A set number of deep breaths at a steady pace, then breathe out and hold for as long as you like.
• Press any button to end the hold, then take one full breath and hold it to recover.
• The hold is a dive: the water gets deeper every 15 seconds and bubbles rise.
• A fish, a jellyfish, a turtle and a whale swim by at 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 and 3:00.
• The Sea log keeps every animal you have met.

BOX BREATHING AND CALM PATTERNS
• Box breathing 4-4-4-4
• 4 7 8 breathing: in 4, hold 7, out 8
• Slow breathing: 5.5 seconds in, 5.5 seconds out
• One pattern of your own: set in, hold, out and rest on the watch

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• After every session a dive profile: one V per round, as deep as your hold.
• Your deepest round is marked; a new personal best shows in coral.
• The tide screen shows your best breath hold per day over 8 weeks.

BREATHE WITH YOUR EYES CLOSED
• Every phase has its own vibration, so you can follow the breathing without looking.
• Vibration can be turned off.

SAVED IN GARMIN CONNECT
• Each session is saved as a breathing activity, one lap per round.
• Your hold time per lap and your best hold are saved with it.
• Pause stops the clock; at the end you choose save or discard.

SETTINGS ON THE WATCH
Breaths per round, pace, rounds, recovery hold, your own pattern, vibration on or off. No phone needed.

WHAT EBB IS NOT
Ebb is breathwork and dry breath hold practice on land. It is not a freediving or apnea trainer, it has no CO2 or O2 tables, and it is not a medical device.

HONEST
• One payment in the Garmin store.
• No subscription, no unlock code by e-mail, no account, no phone app.
• No data leaves your watch, except the activity you choose to save to Garmin Connect.
• Ebb will never nag you for a review.

SAFETY
Sit or lie down. Never practise breath holds in or near water, or while driving. Feel dizzy? Stop and breathe normally.

WATCHES
fenix 7, fenix 8 47 mm, epix Pro 47 mm, Forerunner 255, 265 and 965, Venu 2, Venu 3, vivoactive 5.

Questions or ideas: thijs@dortus.com

Tags: breathing, breathwork, breath hold, breath training, box breathing, 4 7 8 breathing, calm breathing, relaxation, breathing exercises, breath holding, dive
