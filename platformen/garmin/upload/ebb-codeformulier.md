## Per vak

**App name**
```
Ebb Breathing & Breath Hold
```

**Application description**
```
Ebb is a breathing app for round Garmin watches. It offers breath hold rounds, calm 4-7-8 breathing and a pattern you set yourself. Every phase is guided by vibration, so you can keep your eyes closed. The screen is drawn as a small sea: the water rises when you breathe in and falls when you breathe out. There are 24 sea animals to collect, earned by coming back each week, never by holding longer. Before every session the app reminds you to sit or lie down, and it is for practice on land only. No extra account and no phone app. Ebb collects nothing; only a saved activity goes to Garmin Connect. One-time purchase.
```

**Reason for consideration**
```
Ebb is a new paid app from a new developer. Many breathing apps in the store show a circle or a timer. Ebb turns the round screen into a porthole on a sea, with drawn sea animals that reward a weekly habit instead of longer holds. Safety is built into the design: a reminder to sit or lie down before every session, a hold that ends by itself after five minutes, and no rewards for holding longer.

We ask for 10 promotion codes to give to a small group of testers with different watches, so we can hear honest feedback and fix problems early. We do not ask for reviews in return. If codes are only meant for existing buyers, we understand.
```
